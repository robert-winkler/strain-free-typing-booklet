# Keyboard Photo Gallery

This chapter showcases various keyboards, builds, and workspace setups to give you a visual sense of the diversity in mechanical keyboards, especially in the ergonomic and split keyboard space.

## Split Keyboards

Split keyboards separate the keys into two halves, allowing you to position each half independently for better ergonomics.

### Corne Keyboards

The Corne (also known as Crkbd) is a popular 40% split keyboard with a columnar staggered layout.
